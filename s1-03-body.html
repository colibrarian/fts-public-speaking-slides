Part 1 · Three anchors

Before, while, to connect

Before you speak

Long exhale

Breathe in for 4, out for 6. Twice.

While you speak

Feel your feet

Stand on both feet. Weight in the middle.

To connect

Anchor points

Pick a few spots in the room: a face, a forehead, the space above people's heads. Move between them.
