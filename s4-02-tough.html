From Thinking to Speaking · How we speak 4 · On the spot

Part 4 · Tough questions

“How do you know that's true?”

Honest tools
  • “I don't know, but here is what I do know.”Calm honesty earns more trust than a made-up answer.
  • “Could you clarify what you mean by…?”Make sure you answer the real question.
  • “You're right that… and…”Agree with the part that is true.
Dodges to spot
  • Changing the subjectTalking about something easier.
  • Attacking the person“Who are you to ask?”
  • Answering a different questionThe one you wish they had asked.
