Part 2 · Three parts, one idea

Three parts. One idea.

We use the same structure for the team videos.

1 · first 10 seconds

Hook

A question, a surprising fact or a short scene. Why should I keep listening?

2 · about 40 seconds

One idea

One idea, not five. Hold it up with a story and a fact.

3 · last 10 seconds

Call to action

What should the listener do, think or feel now?
