First write three lines on a sticky note.
Then read it out or hand it to the speaker.

1

What worked

Name one specific moment.

“What worked for me was…”

2

What could be stronger

One thing, said as a next step.

“It could be even stronger if…”

3

One question

Something you want to know from the speaker.

“I'm curious about…”

About the speech, not the person
